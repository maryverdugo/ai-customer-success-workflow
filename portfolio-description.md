# Portfolio Description

## AI Customer Success Workflow

I created this project to demonstrate how I am learning to combine customer-service experience with AI and workflow automation.

The goal is simple: help a Customer Success professional move from a customer inquiry to a structured, human-reviewed action plan faster.

The project focuses on practical AI use rather than replacing the customer relationship. AI handles organization, summarization, and suggestions; the Customer Success professional makes the final judgment and communicates with the customer.

This reflects how I think about technology: use it to reduce repetitive work and improve consistency while keeping people at the center of the experience.
