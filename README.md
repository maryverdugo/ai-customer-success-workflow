# AI Customer Success Workflow

An AI-assisted customer success workflow I created to explore how AI can support customer communication, organization, follow-up, and workflow improvement while keeping human judgment at the center.

## Why I Built This

I have more than 30 years of customer service experience and wanted to combine that experience with the AI and workflow technology I am learning.

This project demonstrates how I would use AI to help a customer success professional:

- Understand a customer's request
- Identify the customer's primary need
- Recognize urgency and sentiment
- Suggest an appropriate next step
- Draft a customer response
- Create a follow-up task
- Capture the customer's outcome
- Use customer insights to improve future interactions

## How the Workflow Works

**Customer Inquiry → AI Analysis → Human Review → Response → Follow-Up → Customer Outcome → Improvement**

### 1. Customer Inquiry

A customer submits a question, concern, or request.

### 2. AI Analysis

AI helps organize the information by identifying:

- Primary customer need
- Desired outcome
- Urgency
- Sentiment
- Suggested next action
- Follow-up questions

### 3. Human Review

The AI recommendation is reviewed by a human before any customer communication is sent.

This keeps judgment, empathy, accuracy, and the customer relationship at the center of the process.

### 4. Response

AI can help create a draft response based on the customer's needs and the information available.

The customer success professional reviews and personalizes the response before sending it.

### 5. Follow-Up

A follow-up task is created so the customer does not get lost after the initial interaction.

### 6. Customer Outcome

The result of the interaction is recorded, such as:

- Issue resolved
- Follow-up required
- Customer needs additional assistance
- Opportunity identified

### 7. Continuous Improvement

The information collected can be used to identify recurring customer questions, process problems, and opportunities to improve the customer experience.

## Example

A customer contacts a company because they are having difficulty completing workflow tasks.

AI can help summarize the request, identify the customer's primary need and urgency, suggest a follow-up question, and draft a response.

The customer success professional then reviews the information, communicates with the customer, and determines the appropriate next step.
